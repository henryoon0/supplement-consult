# 사우나(온열요법) (sauna-heat-exposure)

이럴 때 찾는다: 혈행, 근육이완, 피로회복, 디톡스

## 팟캐스트에서 나온 말 (관점일 뿐, 추천 근거 아님)
- [추천] Rhonda Patrick: Patrick says there is no reason to use a sauna at 212°F, considers 190°F a high temperature, and personally uses 180°F. ([영상 0분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=32))
- [원리] Rhonda Patrick: A 20-minute hot bath at 104 degrees Fahrenheit with the shoulders submerged has been associated with increased brain-derived neurotrophic factor and improved blood pressure. ([영상 36분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=2182))
- [용량·타이밍] Rhonda Patrick: Infrared sauna may require about 45 to 60 minutes to produce cardiovascular effects comparable to those of a hotter sauna, though evidence for equivalent effects is limited. ([영상 37분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=2253))
- [원리] Rhonda Patrick: Observational data associate more frequent sauna use with lower all-cause and cardiovascular mortality, stroke risk, and hypertension risk, although these associations do not establish causation. ([영상 31분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=1904))
- [용량·타이밍] Rhonda Patrick: The speaker describes 20 minutes as a sauna-session sweet spot and says that using a sauna at least two times a week may provide some benefits. ([영상 35분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=2124))
- [원리] Rhonda Patrick: Sauna exposure at 163°F for about 30 minutes can increase heat shock proteins by 50% over baseline. ([영상 41분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=2486))
- [추천] Rhonda Patrick: Patrick recommends keeping sauna temperatures at or below 190°F, saying there is no evidence that going hotter is beneficial and that very high temperatures may be harmful. ([영상 44분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=2690))
- [원리] Rhonda Patrick: Deliberate heat exposure such as sauna raises heart rate and produces physiological responses similar to moderate-intensity exercise, and one comparison found similar responses after about 15 minutes of sauna or stationary cycling. ([영상 27분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=1652))
- [경험담] Rhonda Patrick: Observational data cited in the excerpt found that men using the sauna two to three times a week had about a 22% lower rate of sudden cardiac death, and those using it four to seven times a week had about a 63% lower rate, compared with men using it once a week. ([영상 30분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=1836))
- [원리] Rhonda Patrick: Sauna or other deliberate heat exposure alongside exercise is described as improving cardiovascular health, endothelial function, and arterial compliance more than exercise alone. ([영상 45분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=2729))
- [원리] Rhonda Patrick: Heat shock proteins remain activated for about 48 hours, and more frequent heat exposure or exercise is said to elicit that response. ([영상 45분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=2743))
- [추천] Rhonda Patrick: She recommends deliberate heat exposure and says people without sauna access can use hot baths kept at about 104° Fahrenheit. ([영상 58분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=3502))
