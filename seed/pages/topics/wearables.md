# 웨어러블 기기 (wearables)

이럴 때 찾는다: 수면추적, 심박수, 운동량, 건강관리

## 팟캐스트에서 나온 말 (관점일 뿐, 추천 근거 아님)
- [주의점] Michael Pollan: The speaker says glucose meters and step counters can encourage people to change their behavior, such as exercising after meals, but warns that wearable data can become a source of anxiety and compulsive attention. ([영상 131분](https://youtu.be/5QQun2pDQEs?t=7873))
- [원리] Tim Spector: Wearables have made it possible to measure sleep quality and duration more directly, providing better scientific data. ([영상 43분](https://youtu.be/bByTKLZcI20?t=2587))
- [원리] Brigham Buhler: The speaker’s care model uses wearables to track, diagnose, and optimize a patient’s health in real time. ([영상 39분](https://youtu.be/gU6yFAeDsJ0?t=2346))
- [주의점] Andy Galpin: Wearables can measure respiratory rate, but there is no industry standard for how it is calculated, so trends from the same device are more useful than comparing absolute values across devices. ([영상 53분](https://youtu.be/mfUO1CaEMBw?t=3232))
- [추천] Andy Galpin: Andy Galpin says HRV scores differ by device and should be used to track personal progress or regression rather than compared across devices or between friends. ([영상 55분](https://youtu.be/mfUO1CaEMBw?t=3333))
- [추천] Andy Galpin: The breathing rate that produces the highest HRV-related response varies by person, so he recommends identifying an individual resonance frequency rather than using one rate for everyone. ([영상 116분](https://youtu.be/mfUO1CaEMBw?t=6965))
- [주의점] Andy Galpin: He distinguishes the HRV measure being optimized in resonance testing from the highest score shown on wearables, identifying low-frequency power as the relevant measure. ([영상 116분](https://youtu.be/mfUO1CaEMBw?t=7012))
