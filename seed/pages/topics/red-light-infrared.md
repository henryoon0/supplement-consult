# 적외선 광테라피 (red-light-infrared)

이럴 때 찾는다: 피부, 회복, 염증, 통증

## 팟캐스트에서 나온 말 (관점일 뿐, 추천 근거 아님)
- [주의점] Rhonda Patrick: Infrared saunas do not produce the same effects as hot saunas over the same amount of time, but longer sessions may produce similar effects. ([영상 36분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=2218))
- [추천] Rhonda Patrick: She suggests that infrared sauna sessions may need to last about 45 to 60 minutes to obtain the effects discussed, while noting that evidence is limited. ([영상 38분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=2281))
