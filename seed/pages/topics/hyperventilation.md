# 과호흡 (hyperventilation)

이럴 때 찾는다: 호흡법, 산소포화도

## 팟캐스트에서 나온 말 (관점일 뿐, 추천 근거 아님)
- [원리] Andy Galpin: Chronic hyperventilation lowers blood CO2, which can cause hypocapnia and disrupt pH balance, blood flow, oxygenation, and electrolyte or hydration regulation. ([영상 50분](https://youtu.be/mfUO1CaEMBw?t=3011))
- [원리] Andy Galpin: Chronic overbreathing can become a self-sustaining habit associated with stress and may contribute to health and performance problems. ([영상 52분](https://youtu.be/mfUO1CaEMBw?t=3167))
- [주의점] Andy Galpin: Andy Galpin says a respiratory rate above 16 breaths per minute is associated with various health concerns and may indicate chronic overbreathing, though chronic hyperventilation is a medical diagnosis. ([영상 69분](https://youtu.be/mfUO1CaEMBw?t=4176))
- [추천] Andy Galpin: Galpin generally considers respiratory rates of 10 to 14 breaths per minute acceptable, asks follow-up questions at 15, and treats rates above 16 as a high priority. ([영상 70분](https://youtu.be/mfUO1CaEMBw?t=4221))
- [주의점] Andy Galpin: The speaker says that hyperventilation-only breathing is unlikely to reduce stress and anxiety over the long term, though it may be part of a broader practice. ([영상 101분](https://youtu.be/mfUO1CaEMBw?t=6065))
- [원리] Andy Galpin: Galpin says hyperventilation can be used to increase energy and can acutely cause tingling, sweating, and a rise in temperature. ([영상 98분](https://youtu.be/mfUO1CaEMBw?t=5904))
