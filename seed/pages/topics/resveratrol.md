# 레스베라트롤 (resveratrol)

이럴 때 찾는다: 항산화, 노화, 심혈관

## 팟캐스트에서 나온 말 (관점일 뿐, 추천 근거 아님)
- [반론] guest: In a clinical trial of people with peripheral artery disease, adding resveratrol to NR did not improve the six-minute walk test and appeared to block the benefit seen with NR alone. ([영상 69분](https://youtu.be/ELcVYRJJdK4?t=4156))
- [추천] guest: The guest says resveratrol is not necessary for people seeking to boost NAD and that it does not increase SIRT1 activity. ([영상 82분](https://youtu.be/ELcVYRJJdK4?t=4932))
- [주의점] host: The host says resveratrol appeared to negate some benefits of an NAD precursor in peripheral artery disease. ([영상 81분](https://youtu.be/ELcVYRJJdK4?t=4883))
