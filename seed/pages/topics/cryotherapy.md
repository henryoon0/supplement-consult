# 냉동요법 (cryotherapy)

이럴 때 찾는다: 회복, 염증, 통증, 피로

## 팟캐스트에서 나온 말 (관점일 뿐, 추천 근거 아님)
- [경험담] Rhonda Patrick: In a small study of people with major depressive disorder, two minutes of cryotherapy at minus 256° Fahrenheit five times a week for two weeks was associated with a substantial reduction in Hamilton depression scores compared with a sham control. ([영상 49분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=2953))
- [경험담] Rhonda Patrick: She has tried cryotherapy several times but personally prefers cold-water immersion, which she finds more robust because the water contacts the skin. ([영상 49분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=2958))
- [추천] Rhonda Patrick: Cold exposure around resistance training can blunt muscle hypertrophy, so it is better to use it on recovery or endurance-training days, or many hours before or after training. ([영상 56분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=3360))
