# 수면 루틴 (sleep-routine)

## 팟캐스트에서 나온 말 (관점일 뿐, 추천 근거 아님)
- [추천] Andy Galpin: Keeping a consistent sleep schedule is often the first step the speaker examines when someone has poor HRV, and HRV almost always rises when sleep timing becomes consistent. ([영상 60분](https://youtu.be/mfUO1CaEMBw?t=3656))
- [주의점] Andy Galpin: The speaker advises against making decisions about whether to exercise or sleep based on a single day's reading from a cheap consumer wearable. ([영상 60분](https://youtu.be/mfUO1CaEMBw?t=3612))
