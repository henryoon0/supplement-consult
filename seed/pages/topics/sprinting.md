# 스프린트(전력질주) (sprinting)

이럴 때 찾는다: 근력, 심폐지구력, 다이어트

## 팟캐스트에서 나온 말 (관점일 뿐, 추천 근거 아님)
- [추천] Rhonda Patrick: The speaker says that sprinting up stairs is one way to use everyday situations to raise the heart rate through brief exercise bursts. ([영상 24분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=1455))
- [경험담] Rhonda Patrick: Rhonda Patrick includes sprinting as one component of her CrossFit workouts, which she does for about three hours per week. ([영상 87분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=5244))
- [용량·타이밍] Stacy Sims: Sprint interval training involves going as hard as possible for 30 seconds or less, with two to five minutes of recovery between intervals. ([영상 2분](https://youtu.be/VcmY1SAtneM?t=130))
- [추천] Stacy Sims: Stacy Sims recommends including one or two sprint interval sessions among two to three high-intensity sessions per week. ([영상 4분](https://youtu.be/VcmY1SAtneM?t=280))
- [용량·타이밍] Stacy Sims: A sprint interval session can follow a short heavy-lifting session and may use intervals of 30 seconds or less with adequate recovery. ([영상 8분](https://youtu.be/VcmY1SAtneM?t=493))
- [추천] Stacy Sims: Sprint intervals can be incorporated into one or two weekly strength sessions using battle ropes or explosive kettlebell swings. ([영상 8분](https://youtu.be/VcmY1SAtneM?t=531))
