# 고강도 인터벌 트레이닝 (hiit)

이럴 때 찾는다: 다이어트, 체지방, 심폐지구력

## 팟캐스트에서 나온 말 (관점일 뿐, 추천 근거 아님)
- [원리] Rhonda Patrick: HIIT uses short, hard intervals followed by recovery, and may improve cardiorespiratory fitness in people who do not respond to moderate-intensity exercise. ([영상 8분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=501))
- [원리] Rhonda Patrick: High-intensity interval training increases mitochondrial biogenesis in muscle more robustly than deliberate cold exposure. ([영상 54분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=3250))
- [용량·타이밍] Stacy Sims: HIIT is described as a 40-to-45-minute workout with warm-up and cool-down, using one-to-four-minute intervals at 80–85% effort and variable recovery to accumulate around 18 to 20 minutes in that zone. ([영상 0분](https://youtu.be/VcmY1SAtneM?t=29))
- [원리] Stacy Sims: Sprint interval training is a subset of HIIT that uses all-out efforts of 30 seconds or less with two to five minutes of recovery, and Sims says it can improve insulin sensitivity and reduce the need to store visceral fat. ([영상 2분](https://youtu.be/VcmY1SAtneM?t=130))
- [용량·타이밍] Stacy Sims: Stacy Sims recommends two to three high-intensity sessions per week, with the specific mix of HIIT and sprint intervals varying by the plan. ([영상 4분](https://youtu.be/VcmY1SAtneM?t=280))
- [주의점] Stacy Sims: She cautions that workouts that drift into moderate intensity may increase sympathetic drive without producing the intended benefits, and suggests stopping or switching activities when the target intensity cannot be reached. ([영상 5분](https://youtu.be/VcmY1SAtneM?t=309))
- [주의점] Andy Galpin: High-intensity interval training appears to be the most effective of the exercise types mentioned for the specific marker, but excessive daily maximal effort may eventually worsen it. ([영상 93분](https://youtu.be/mfUO1CaEMBw?t=5623))
- [추천] Andy Galpin: Adding higher-intensity training may improve HRV, respiratory rate, and resting heart rate when someone is inactive or does little or moderate exercise. ([영상 94분](https://youtu.be/mfUO1CaEMBw?t=5656))
