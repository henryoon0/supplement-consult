# 신경가소성 (neuroplasticity)

이럴 때 찾는다: 뇌건강, 학습, 기억력

- [원리] unknown: High-volume, low-quality, quick social-media videos and infinite scrolling are said to rewire the brain in ways that worsen stress, mental health, attention, cognition, distractibility, irritability, and complex problem-solving. ([영상 6분](https://youtu.be/EScgrk7oEwU?t=413))
- [원리] unknown: The speaker says that forming new brain circuits through neuroplasticity takes eight weeks. ([영상 29분](https://youtu.be/EScgrk7oEwU?t=1763))
- [원리] unknown: Social media patterns are said to reshape and rewire the brain through neuroplasticity and alter brain waves in ways that mimic addictive behaviors. ([영상 32분](https://youtu.be/EScgrk7oEwU?t=1976))
- [원리] guest: Lactate signals the brain to increase brain-derived neurotrophic factor, which supports neuron growth, learning, memory, and neuroplasticity. ([영상 18분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=1087))
- [원리] guest: Vigorous-intensity exercise raises lactate the most among the exercise intensities discussed and is associated with the greatest impulse control. ([영상 19분](https://youtu.be/OCddz_0NrEE?t=1170))
- [원리] unknown: Exercise is thought to increase BDNF, which supports neuroplasticity by helping neurons form new connections. ([영상 46분](https://youtu.be/Xm_PHZXGe-w?t=2768))
- [원리] Andrew Huberman: The speaker says that enriched-environment experiments may show benefits because standard laboratory cages deprive animals of what they naturally need, rather than because added toys uniquely enhance brain development. ([영상 40분](https://youtu.be/hlOA8ObQJXo?t=2423))
- [원리] Richard Davidson: The agitation of struggling with a difficult problem or passage can stimulate neuroplasticity, whereas an easy task gives the brain less reason to change. ([영상 51분](https://youtu.be/hlOA8ObQJXo?t=3088))
