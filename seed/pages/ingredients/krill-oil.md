## 커뮤니티 반응 (Reddit 요약)
Reddit users are increasingly skeptical of krill oil due to low EPA/DHA content relative to price and growing environmental concerns about krill harvesting. While some appreciate the phospholipid form and astaxanthin content for better absorption, many are switching to high-quality fish oil or algae oil alternatives that provide more omega-3s per dollar and less ecological impact.
