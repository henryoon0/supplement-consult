## 커뮤니티 반응 (Reddit 요약)
Reddit consensus is deeply split on multivitamins - many view them as beneficial 'insurance policies' for nutritional gaps, while others criticize popular brands like Centrum for poor absorption and quality. Users consistently emphasize that benefits are mainly seen when addressing actual deficiencies, not as general health boosters.
