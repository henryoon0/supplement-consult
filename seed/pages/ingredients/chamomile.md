## 커뮤니티 반응 (Reddit 요약)
Chamomile is widely viewed as an effective, gentle natural remedy primarily for sleep and anxiety, with users appreciating both its therapeutic effects and ritual aspects. While most experiences are positive, users seek clearer guidance on dosing, safety interactions, and preventing tolerance, particularly when comparing tea versus supplement forms.
