## 커뮤니티 반응 (Reddit 요약)
Pea protein is viewed as an effective but inferior alternative to whey protein, primarily chosen by those with dietary restrictions or dairy sensitivities. The main consensus is that while pea protein can deliver similar muscle-building results, it suffers from poor taste/texture and requires larger servings, making it mainly suitable for vegans or those who cannot tolerate whey.
