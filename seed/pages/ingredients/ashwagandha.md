## 커뮤니티 반응 (Reddit 요약)
Reddit discussions reveal Ashwagandha as a highly polarizing supplement with genuine benefits for anxiety and sleep, but significant safety concerns around liver toxicity and emotional blunting. Users emphasize the importance of quality brands, proper cycling, and low doses, with many ultimately preferring alternatives like magnesium glycinate and L-theanine for similar benefits without the risks.
