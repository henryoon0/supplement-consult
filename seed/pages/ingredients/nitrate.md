## 커뮤니티 반응 (Reddit 요약)
Nitrate supplements show promise for athletic performance and cardiovascular health, but users struggle with cost, taste, and GI issues. There's significant confusion about different nitrate types and safety, with many preferring whole food sources like beets and arugula over processed supplements.
