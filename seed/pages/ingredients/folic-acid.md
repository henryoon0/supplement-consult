## 커뮤니티 반응 (Reddit 요약)
Reddit discussions reveal deep concerns about folic acid supplementation, particularly around MTHFR gene mutations affecting 40-60% of the population who cannot properly process synthetic folic acid. While folic acid is widely recommended for pregnancy to prevent neural tube defects, many users are shifting toward methylated forms or questioning whether fortification in foods is causing more harm
