## 커뮤니티 반응 (Reddit 요약)
Resistant starch is viewed as a promising gut health supplement with legitimate benefits for blood sugar control and microbiome support, but users struggle with sourcing therapeutic doses and managing initial digestive side effects. The market appears underserved for high-concentration, easily accessible resistant starch products that can deliver the 30g doses used in clinical research.
