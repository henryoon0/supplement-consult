## 커뮤니티 반응 (Reddit 요약)
Reddit users are divided on probiotics, with many preferring fermented foods like kefir and kimchi over expensive supplements. While some report significant benefits for digestive and mental health, others emphasize the lack of regulation and scientific consensus, leading to a preference for food-based sources as more reliable and cost-effective alternatives.
