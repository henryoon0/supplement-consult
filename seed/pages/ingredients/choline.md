## 커뮤니티 반응 (Reddit 요약)
Reddit discussions reveal choline as a polarizing supplement - users either experience significant cognitive benefits or severe depression/anxiety. Those with genetic mutations often require higher doses and see dramatic improvements, while others struggle with mood side effects that can be mitigated with B5 supplementation. Many prefer food sources like eggs over supplements due to safety concern
